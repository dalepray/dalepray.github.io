Privacy Policy – Dale Ray REALTOR (TikTok App)

Effective date: September 28, 2026

1. Who we are

"Dale Ray REALTOR" is a private tool operated by Dale Patrick Ray, REALTOR®, Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices C. Dan Joyner, REALTORS®, Greenville, South Carolina. It is used only to publish real estate content to Dale Patrick Ray's own TikTok account.

2. What information the app receives

When a TikTok account is connected, TikTok shares only what the account owner approves on TikTok's permission screen. This may include:

  • Basic profile information (such as display name, avatar, and account ID)
  • An access token that allows the app to upload and publish videos to that account
  • Information about videos posted through the app (such as upload status)

3. How the information is used

This information is used only to upload and publish videos and captions to the connected TikTok account and to confirm that posts went through. It is not used for advertising or profiling.

4. Sharing

The information is not sold or rented to anyone. Access tokens are stored by the third-party automation service used to connect to TikTok (Composio), only so posts can be published. Information may be disclosed if required by law.

5. Storage and security

Reasonable measures are used to protect this information. Access tokens are kept only as long as the TikTok account stays connected.

6. Your choices

You can revoke this app's access at any time from your TikTok account settings. After access is revoked, the app can no longer post to your account, and the stored access token stops working.

7. Children

This app is not directed to anyone under 18.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
